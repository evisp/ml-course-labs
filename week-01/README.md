# Week 01 · Problem framing and baselines

**The question:** at the moment a customer places an order, can we predict whether it will arrive late?

In this lab you:

- turn "late" into a precise target, and see why that is a decision
- look at how lateness changes over time and across Brazil
- build three baselines, from doing nothing to a tiny decision tree
- watch a leak make a useless model look brilliant

Open `lab.ipynb`, then save your own copy as `my-lab.ipynb` and work in that.

Uses two tables: orders and customers. Needs the data from `python data/download.py`.
